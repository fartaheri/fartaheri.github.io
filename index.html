01 / My story

A life connected
to learning.

I spent more than 20 years teaching high school students in Iran, including history and philosophy. Alongside classroom teaching, I supported students who needed extra guidance and encouragement.

Now living in Australia, I want to build on that experience and return to education. I am particularly interested in working with children with disability and additional learning needs, helping them take part in school life with confidence.
